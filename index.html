Questions owners ask

Which suppliers can it read invoices from?

Any printed or PDF invoice. Broadline distributors, cash and carry receipts and most local produce and meat vendors import cleanly. If a line doesn't read, you fix it once and Parline remembers.

Do I need to set par levels before I start?

No. Parline suggests pars from your first four weeks of counts and invoices. You can override any of them at any time.

Does it work with no signal in the cooler?

Yes. Counts save on the phone and sync when you're back in range.

Can my staff count without seeing costs?

Yes. Counters see items and quantities only. Prices, food cost and orders stay with owners and managers.

Does it connect to my POS?

It works without one. Connecting your POS adds sales data, which makes par suggestions sharper for items that swing with the menu.

What happens when the trial ends?

You pick the plan or your account goes read only. We never took a card, so nothing is charged automatically.

Can I get my data out?

Every count, order and price history exports to CSV from settings, whenever you want it.
